## Particle Editor

- **Adjust**: hue and scale of a whole system
- **Child Particles**: add, open, reorder and remove child systems, in the inspector and the graph
- **Hide** and **Solo** on child systems
- **Spawn Shape**: Point, Legacy, Box, Sphere or Cylinder, with **Emit Rotations**
- **Sprite sheet** in the Texture group
- **Effects quality** in the preview's view menu
- Particle shadows at High effects quality and above
- Missile flight in the preview
- **Target handle** for beams and missiles
- Preview: beam meshes, segment beams and sliced emitters
- Curves: playhead and pointer time
- Picking a graph node highlights its row in the inspector
