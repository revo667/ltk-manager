## Patcher

- Updated for League patch 26.20

## Library

- **Table view** replaces the list on the Mods page
- Columns move, resize, sort and hide. The layout is kept across restarts
- **View options**: **Row height**, **Columns** and **Group by**
- **Group by**: folder, champion, map, tag, author, health, on or off, installed, format, storage or
  license
- The mod panel shows the mod under the pointer. **Pin** keeps it on one mod
- A click on a row turns its mod on or off
- A drag down the checkbox column selects rows
- A drag on a row changes the load order
- **Move to folder** on a mod's menu and on the selection bar

## Workshop

- **Table view** replaces **List view** in the project list
- Project columns: **Location**, **Modified** and **Opened**
- List footer: project count, selection and view control
- `N selected` opens **Test**, **Pack**, **Delete** and **Clear selection**
- Search bar and history arrows are in the title bar
- A project's problems badge, **Test** and **Pack** are in the status bar
- Panes float over the editor: **Float** in a tab's right-click menu, **Dock** to return
- The objects browser lists objects that only a project declares
- Game index search starts in **Whole game**

## Particle Editor

- Preview: emitter timing, spawn counts, particle motion and draw order follow the game's rules
- A timeline lane states why its emitter does not spawn
- **Source** group: emission mesh and emission surface. The preview spawns particles on them
- Inspector notes report emission settings the game ignores
- Preview draws stencil masks
- **Stencil** group: **Stencil Mode** and the mask
- **Emitter > Mask** sets both in one step
- **Show > Stencil masks** tints masks in the viewport
- Inspector toolbar: **Add property**, **View** menu and **Emitter** menu
- Graph: minimap, flat beam emitter previews, spacing between overlapping nodes

## Skin Preview

- **Skeleton** and **Physics** panes edit pose modifiers, sockets and colliders
- Preview simulates dynamics chain, spring, conform to path, joint orientation and lock root
  orientation
- A viewport gizmo moves and turns a socket

## Interface

- Title bar: **Home**, **Mods** and **Workshop** are buttons, and icons in a narrow window
- Error notifications stay until dismissed
- At most four notifications show at once
- Dialogs fit the window. Content scrolls in a short window
- One set of sizes and styles for buttons, fields, dropdowns, menus and tooltips

### Changed
