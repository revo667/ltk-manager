## Audio

- `.wpk`, `.bnk` and `.wem` files open in the Workshop, from a project or from the game
- **Media** view: play each sound, see its waveform and save it to a file
- **Events** view: every event of a bank as one tree, with its containers, sounds and names
- Edit the properties of a sound or a container with knobs, and hear the change in the preview
- **New event**, rename, duplicate and delete, and the bank's units update their event lists with the edit
- **New sound**, random, sequence and switch containers, **Wrap in container**, cut and paste, and **Import .wem…**
- **Replace audio...** takes a `.wem`, or a `.wav`, `.ogg` or `.flac` once **Settings > Workshop > Audio > Wwise console** is set
- **Played by** column on events shows which voice line rule plays each one
- Menu bar on audio files, and `?` lists every key and gesture

## Voice Lines

- `ContextualActionData` objects open in a **Voice lines** view, with the situations, rules, conditions and audiences as a table
- **Resolve as** picks the skin, and each line shows the tag it resolved through or a **Missing** warning
- Play one line, or simulate a situation for an audience, a target and a spell
- Edit a rule's name, line, chance, weight and priority in the list, and every other field in the inspector
- **Add chance**, **Remove chance** and **Pick a random rule** in the list's menus
- Add, duplicate, delete and reorder rules, and drag an event from a bank onto a rule to make it play that event
- **Copy for this skin** gives a skin its own copy of voice lines that other skins share

## Sound in Previews

- The skin viewport plays the sounds of its clips and effects. Sound is off by default, and the speaker switch in its toolbar turns it on
- The VFX preview and the ability preview play their sounds

## Bin Editor

- Sound fields such as `mSoundName` and `SoundOnCreate` suggest events from the skin's banks, then matching events from the rest of the game
- **Declared objects** in the Objects browser lists what your game data declarations create, remove or edit

## Library

- Mod health: **Convert to version 145** repairs an audio bank in an older version
- Mod health: the skinhack repair removes the bank units and tags of another skin, and the base skin's audio plays in their place

### Changed

- Game data declarations are YAML only. Opening a project converts its JSON and TOML manifests and source files to YAML
- An edit of an object that a `source` module creates is written to that source file, and undo restores it

### Fixed

- Game data edits keep their property types on a game patch newer than the meta database
